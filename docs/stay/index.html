House notes

A family home before it is a rental. We live downstairs. Tap a note to read more.

SoundQuiet after 10 pm

Please keep music low, and take conversations indoors after 10 at night. Sound travels faster through solid walls than you would expect.

SmokingBalcony & rooftop only

The balcony and the rooftop, please. Not the hall, not the bedroom. Ashtrays are out there, and the fort looks better from up top anyway.

DrinkingAllowed, mind the floor

Allowed, and left to your own judgement. Please mind the floor — it is old Makrana marble and it remembers everything.

VisitorsBooked guests only

Only the guests on the booking come inside. Anyone else, please meet outside.

LeavingCheck-out by 10 am

Check-out by 10 in the morning, and please do keep to it. There is one flat, and it has to be made properly for whoever comes next.

Spills & breaksTell us straight away

Tell us straight away, whatever it is. Accidents are completely fine — it is the ones left sitting that become a problem.

Jodhpur Guide

Where we actually go. Every place opens in Google Maps with directions from our door.

Into the old city? Leave the car

For the Clock Tower, Nav Chokia, Brahmpuri or anywhere in the blue city, skip your own car or scooty. The lanes jam and there is nowhere to park. A taxi or an auto drops you right there.
