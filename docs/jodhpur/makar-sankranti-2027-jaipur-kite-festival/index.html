Makar Sankranti 2027: Jaipur's Kite Day, and a Quiet Jodhpur

Makar Sankranti is 14–15 January 2027. Where the kites really fly in Rajasthan (Jaipur), the official Jal Mahal festival, Ahmedabad's Uttarayan, and how Jodhpur fits in.

By Ajay, Hari Om Niwas, Jodhpur · 18 November 2026

If you want kites, go to Jaipur. On Makar Sankranti the whole city is on its roofs from morning, music playing, the sky full of kites, and at night it lets off fireworks like it's Diwali.

Quick facts

Date14 January 2027 is kite day. The ritual Sankranti falls on 15 January this year (sun's transit 9:14 pm on the 14th, per Drik Panchang).

What it's really like

Jaipur on Sankranti is bonkers. It's like Rakhi in the day and Diwali at night: kites and music all day, then firecrackers once it's dark. People book a friend's or a stranger's place just for a good rooftop and fly the whole day. Ahmedabad is the same. Jodhpur is very normal. You'll see a few people flying kites, but nothing like a celebration. — Ajay

So plan your Sankranti in Jaipur, and come to Jodhpur before or after for the fort and the old city without the crowds.

The story you'll hear

Ask around and you'll hear a few versions. These are what locals say, not textbook history:

  • The sun turns north today (Uttarayan), so the cold days are ending and good days begin.
  • In the Mahabharata, Bhishma waited on his bed of arrows for this day before he died.
  • You eat til (sesame) and gud (jaggery) to keep warm and to "speak sweetly" all year.
  • Flying kites gets everyone out into the winter sun.

Doing it in Jaipur

  • Get a roof. Book a stay with rooftop access, or go to Jal Mahal for the official event.
  • Stay up for the night. Sky lanterns and fireworks follow the kites.
  • Buy kites the day before. Get a few kites and one reel (charkhi).
  • Tape your index finger. The string (manjha) is coated and cuts. Loose string across roads is dangerous on a two-wheeler.

Adding Jodhpur to the trip

Jaipur to Jodhpur is about 5–6 hours by road or train. Mid-January is a good time for Jodhpur: cool, clear days and fewer people at Mehrangarh than in the Sacred Spirit Festival weeks.

A simple plan: Jaipur on 13–14 Jan for the kites, then Jodhpur from the 15th for the fort, Jaswant Thada, Mandore and the old city.
