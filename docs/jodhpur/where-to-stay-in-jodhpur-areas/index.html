Where to Stay in Jodhpur: Best Areas, Honestly Compared

Old city, Mahamandir, Ratanada or Sardarpura? A short, honest local comparison of where to stay in Jodhpur.

By Ajay, Hari Om Niwas, Jodhpur · 18 November 2026

Jodhpur is small. A 15-minute drive is the difference between the lanes under the fort and a quiet family street. Here is how the areas compare.

Old city

This is the walled city: the Clock Tower, Sardar Market, the stepwells and the blue houses.

  • Yes: you can walk to the fort, the markets and Toorji ka Jhalra, with rooftop views everywhere.
  • But: you often carry your bags up lanes a car can't enter, parking is hard, and rooftop restaurants run late.
  • And honestly: many old city lanes are dirty. Open drains overflow, rubbish piles up, and guests tell us about the smell. If that's outside your door, there's nothing you can do about it.
Jodhpur isn't a big town. Most people stay 1–3 nights and see the fort, Jaswant Thada, Mandore, Umaid Bhawan and the old city. You don't need to live inside the old city to see it. Stay in Paota, Mahamandir or Ratanada, take a cab or a Rapido in, and walk the old city on your own terms. No parking, no traffic, and it's much cleaner. — Ajay

Mahamandir

A settled residential area north-east of the old city, named after its big temple.

  • Yes: quiet at night, cars reach the door, and you see everyday Jodhpur.
  • But: you won't step straight into the blue lanes, and there are fewer tourist cafés within walking distance.

Ratanada

The palace and airport side, with wide roads, quieter streets and most of the large chain hotels. Close to Umaid Bhawan and good for an early flight.

Sardarpura and Paota

Normal city life, with markets, sweet shops and places locals eat. Paota is the closest of all these to the old city by road.

Ask before you book, anywhere

  • Can a car reach the door? If not, how far is the walk, and is it uphill?
  • Is there a usable roof? That's where Jodhpur's sunsets happen.
  • Is there food at the house? It saves evening trips in and out of the old city.

Getting around is easy from any of these areas: Ola, Uber, Rapido and autos are everywhere.
