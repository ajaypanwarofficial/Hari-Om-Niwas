From our kitchen

This is a home that cooks.

We serve what we eat. Everything is made in our own kitchen, the way it has been made in this family for years: real home food, not a tourist version of it. Nothing sits ready under a lamp, so the good things take a little time.

Pick from the menu in the room, send it on WhatsApp, and it comes up hot.

  • Aloo paranthaThe one guests ask for again. With dahi and ghar ka achaar.
  • Dal DhokliThe underrated gem of Jodhpur. Soft wheat ribbons simmered in the dal.
  • Dal Baati ChurmaEat until you feel good. That is the only correct portion.
  • Jodhpur ThaliGatta, rabodi and what the market gave us, with khichdi, kadhi and ghee gur churma.
